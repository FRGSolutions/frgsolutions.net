How your home gets built

Eight phases, one builder, and you know where things stand at every step.

  1. Pre-constructionSite visit, goals, budget range, and a review of your lot and any existing plans.
  2. Plan review and pricingDetailed scope and construction budget, with allowances and exclusions written out.
  3. PermittingWe coordinate with your design team and the local building department through approval.
  4. Site work and foundationGrading, excavation, utilities, and the foundation your home sits on.
  5. Framing and dry-inWalls, roof, windows, and doors, until the structure is closed to the weather.
  6. Systems and inspectionsPlumbing, electrical, and mechanical rough-in, inspected before walls close.
  7. Interior and exterior finishesDrywall, cabinets, countertops, tile, flooring, paint, and exterior finishes.
  8. Final inspection and keysFinal walkthrough, punch list complete, final inspection signed off, and the keys in your hand.
